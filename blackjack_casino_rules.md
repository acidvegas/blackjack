In a casino blackjack table with a dealer and up to about 7 players, everyone plays individually against the dealer, not against each other. The other players at the table do not affect your bet or your winnings. Here is the basic flow of how a hand works.

First, everyone places their bet.
Each player puts chips in the betting circle in front of their seat. The dealer will wait until all players have placed bets. Each person chooses their own bet amount. You do not have to match anyone else's bet.

Example:
Player 1 bets $10
Player 2 bets $50
Player 3 bets $25
All bets can be different.

After bets are placed, no one can change their bet, except in special cases later (like doubling or splitting).

Next, the dealer deals the cards.
Each player gets 2 cards face up.
The dealer gets 2 cards:

one face up

one face down (called the “hole card”).

Now players take turns making decisions, starting from the player on the dealer’s left and going clockwise.

Each player can choose actions such as:

Hit – take another card.
Stand – keep your current hand.
Double Down – double your original bet and receive exactly one more card.
Split – if your two cards are the same value, you can split them into two hands by placing another bet equal to your original bet.

Important:
You cannot place random new bets during the hand. The only time you add more money is when the rules allow it (double down or split).

After all players finish their turns, the dealer plays.
The dealer reveals the hidden card and follows fixed rules:

Dealer must hit until at least 17

Dealer must stand on 17 or higher

The dealer does not make choices like players do.

Finally, the hands are compared.

If your hand is higher than the dealer without going over 21 → you win.

If the dealer is higher → you lose.

If both are the same → it is a push (tie) and you get your bet back.

If you go over 21 → you bust and lose immediately.
