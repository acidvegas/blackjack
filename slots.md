A casino slot machine is essentially a random outcome generator with reels and symbols. You place a bet, spin the reels, and win if certain symbol combinations align on predefined “paylines.”

1. You place a bet

Before spinning, you choose how much money to wager.

Example bet levels:

💰 $1
💰💰 $5
💰💰💰 $10

The bet is deducted each time you spin.

2. The reels spin

Most slots have 3–5 reels. Each reel shows a random symbol after the spin.

Example result:

🍒 🍋 ⭐
🍋 🍒 7️⃣
⭐ 🍋 🍒

Only specific lines count as wins (usually horizontal, sometimes diagonal or zig-zag).

Example winning line (middle row):

🍋 🍒 🍒 🍒 🍋

3. Matching symbols = payout

If enough matching symbols appear on a payline, you win money.

Typical examples:

🍒 🍒 🍒 → small win
🍋 🍋 🍋 → medium win
⭐ ⭐ ⭐ → bigger win
7️⃣ 7️⃣ 7️⃣ → jackpot

Example:

🍒 🍒 🍒
🍋 ⭐ 🍋
⭐ 🍋 ⭐

Top row wins because the cherries match.
